# Sales-performance-analysis

## Project Overview
An interactive sales analytics dashboard designed to monitor business performance and explore revenue trends across products, regions, and sales channels.

## Objectives
- Track key sales performance indicators.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Compare revenue across regions and product categories.
- Evaluate sales channel performance.
- Identify the top five products by revenue.

## Tools Used
- Dashboard tool:  Excel
-Cleaning - Excel
## Key Performance Indicators
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value

## Dashboard Features
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Regional revenue comparison
- Product category performance
- Sales channel analysis
- Top five products by revenue
- Interactive date, product, channel, customer type, and region filters

## Key Findings

1. Central region leads revenue:
The Central region generated approximately $803K, followed by North at $787K, East at $658K, South at $628K, and West at $587K. Central is the strongest region by revenue, while West has the lowest displayed regional total.

2. Computers dominate category revenue:
Computers contribute approximately $2.06M, substantially more than Networking ($292K), Office Supplies ($270K), and Accessories ($86K). This makes computers the leading category in the displayed breakdown.

3. Monthly revenue fluctuates throughout the year:
April records a local peak of approximately $347K, while October is the lowest displayed month at around $237K. November rebounds to $309K before December closes at $270K.

4. Gaming Laptop is the top product by revenue:
Gaming Laptop leads the displayed top five products at approximately $666K. Laptop Pro 14 and Laptop Air 13 follow at $404K and $403K, respectively

5. Sales channel contributions are relatively concentrated:
Corporate Sales and Online appear to be the largest channel contributors, at approximately $1.01M and $1.11M, respectively. Retail Store contributes around $907K, while Marketplace contributes $429K.
